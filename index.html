FAQ

Questions we hear a lot.

Can't find yours? The AI assistant has the same answers, and can pass anything else to the team.

Do you work with small businesses?

Our best fit is 20 to 500 employees. Smaller teams often start with a Process Automation package or a Microsoft 365 / Google Workspace review.

How much does a project cost?

Every project gets a fixed price after a free intro call. As a guide, packages start at $2,800, and Fractional CTO support starts at $3,200 per month.

Can I get a quote today?

We can share starting prices right away. A fixed quote needs a 30-minute intro call, and you'll have it in writing within 3 business days.

How soon can you start?

Usually 2 to 3 weeks after you accept a proposal.

What happens on the intro call?

A 30-minute video call with a consultant. We talk about your goals and current setup, check whether we're a good fit and agree on next steps.

Where are you based?

We're remote-first and headquartered in Austin, Texas, with clients across the US, Canada and the UK. Meetings are scheduled in your time zone within our business hours.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes. We're happy to sign a mutual NDA before discovery.

How do you handle our data and system access?

We use named accounts with the least access needed, require multi-factor authentication and remove our access when the project ends. We'll never ask for passwords by chat, phone or email.

Are you tied to any vendors?

No. We're vendor-neutral and don't take commissions, so recommendations are based only on what suits you.

Which tools do you work with?

Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, n8n, Make, Zapier, Power Automate, Power BI, Looker Studio, HubSpot and Salesforce integrations, plus core Azure and AWS services.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Every project includes 30 days of support. Optional support plans start at $750 per month.

Do you provide emergency IT support?

No. We don't run a help desk or incident response. If you have an urgent outage or security incident, contact your IT provider; we can refer you to a specialist partner.

What are your payment terms?

Projects are 50% on acceptance and 50% on delivery. Retainers are billed monthly in advance.

Can you train our staff?

Yes. Training is part of AI Readiness & Adoption and every handover, and we run standalone workshops too.

How do I change or cancel an intro call?

Reply to your confirmation email, or ask the assistant to pass the request to our team.
