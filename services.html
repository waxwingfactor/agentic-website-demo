Pricing and payment

  • The intro call is free.
  • Projects are fixed-price, quoted in writing after the intro call. Starting prices on this site are guides, not quotes.
  • Projects are billed 50% on acceptance and 50% on delivery. Retainers are billed monthly in advance.
  • Prices are in US dollars. We don't take reseller commissions.
  • Optional support plans after a project start from $750 per month.

What we don't do

  • Break/fix IT support, help desk or on-site repairs
  • Buying or reselling hardware and software licences
  • Custom mobile app development
  • Cybersecurity incident response (we refer you to a specialist partner)
  • Legal, tax, financial or compliance advice
