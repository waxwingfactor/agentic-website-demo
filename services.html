Services

Services and starting prices

Every engagement is fixed-price and starts with a free 30-minute intro call. Starting prices are guides; your proposal arrives in writing within 3 business days.

IT Strategy & Roadmap

We review your current systems, costs and risks, interview key people, then deliver a prioritised 12-month roadmap with budget ranges and a clear first quarter plan.

Typical length: 4 to 6 weeksPrice: from $6,500Led by: Strategy team

What's included

  • Current-state review of systems, costs and risks
  • Interviews with leadership and key staff
  • Prioritised 12-month roadmap with budget ranges
  • Board-ready summary presentation

Process Automation

We map one or more manual workflows, such as order intake, onboarding or invoicing, and automate them with tools like n8n, Make, Zapier or Power Automate. Every automation ships with documentation and a handover session.

Typical length: 2 to 6 weeksPrice: from $3,500 per workflow packageLed by: Automation & AI team

What's included

  • Process mapping workshop
  • Automation build and testing
  • Error alerts and monitoring
  • Documentation and handover session

AI Readiness & Adoption

We assess where AI can save time or improve service, write a practical AI usage policy, train your staff and run one supervised pilot so you can measure results before scaling.

Typical length: 3 to 5 weeksPrice: from $4,800Led by: Automation & AI team

What's included

  • Use-case assessment and prioritisation
  • AI usage policy for staff
  • Hands-on staff training
  • One supervised pilot with success measures

Microsoft 365 & Google Workspace Optimization

We review licences, sharing settings, admin roles and security settings in Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, fix the quick wins and plan any migration.

Typical length: 2 to 4 weeksPrice: from $2,800Led by: Cloud & Data team

What's included

  • Licence and cost review
  • Security baseline (MFA, sharing, admin roles)
  • Clean-up of users, groups and shared drives
  • Migration plan if needed

Data & Reporting

We connect your existing systems to Power BI or Looker Studio and build a small set of KPI dashboards, with definitions everyone agrees on.

Typical length: 3 to 6 weeksPrice: from $3,900Led by: Cloud & Data team

What's included

  • KPI definition workshop
  • Data connections to your existing systems
  • Up to five dashboards
  • Training for report owners

Fractional CTO

A senior consultant acts as your part-time technology leader: owning the roadmap, managing vendors, reviewing spend and guiding your internal team.

Typical length: Ongoing, minimum 3 monthsPrice: from $3,200 per monthLed by: Strategy team

What's included

  • Roadmap ownership
  • Vendor and contract management
  • Monthly spend and risk review
  • Guidance for your internal IT team

Pricing and payment

  • The intro call is free.
  • Projects are fixed-price, quoted in writing after the intro call. Starting prices on this site are guides, not quotes.
  • Projects are billed 50% on acceptance and 50% on delivery. Retainers are billed monthly in advance.
  • Prices are in US dollars. We don't take reseller commissions.
  • Optional support plans after a project start from $750 per month.

What we don't do

  • Break/fix IT support, help desk or on-site repairs
  • Buying or reselling hardware and software licences
  • Custom mobile app development
  • Cybersecurity incident response (we refer you to a specialist partner)
  • Legal, tax, financial or compliance advice
