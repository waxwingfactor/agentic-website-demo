AI & privacy notice

How our AI assistant works with your information

Plain-language notice for this demo site. A real deployment would have this reviewed for the client's jurisdiction.

You're talking to an AI. The chat, voice and callback assistant on this site is an AI system, not a person. It tells you so at the start of every conversation. You can ask for a person at any time.

What the assistant can and can't do

It can:

  • Answer questions about Tidewell's services, process and pricing guides
  • Help you work out which service fits
  • Book a free 30-minute intro call
  • Pass your request to the right consultant
  • Email you a summary of the services you asked about
  • Connect you with a person when you ask, or when it can't help

It can't:

  • Give a binding quote or agree contract terms
  • Give legal, tax, financial or compliance advice
  • Troubleshoot or access your systems
  • Discuss other clients beyond the published examples
  • Take payments or ask for passwords

Before it books a meeting, saves your details or sends an email, it reads the details back and asks you to confirm.

What we collect

  • The messages you type, or the audio of your call and its transcript.
  • Details you choose to give: name, email, phone number, company and what you need.
  • Technical details needed to run the conversation, such as your time zone and the page you started from.

Recording and consent

Voice and callback calls are recorded and transcribed. The assistant tells you this at the start and asks whether that's okay. If you say no, it ends the call and points you to the chat or email instead. Requesting a callback is consent to one call about your request.

Who processes it

  • Retell AI runs the chat and voice assistant, including speech recognition, the language model and call recordings.
  • n8n runs the workflows that check the calendar, save requests and prepare emails.
  • Google hosts the meeting calendar and sends internal notification emails.

How long we keep it

Chats and calls are processed by our AI provider and stored for 30 days so our team can follow up. Calls are recorded and transcribed. Please don't share passwords, payment card numbers or health information. Requests you save (meetings, enquiries, handovers) stay in our records for the length of the demo.

Your choices

  • Ask for a person at any point in a conversation.
  • Ask us to delete your conversation and details by asking the assistant to pass a deletion request to the team.
  • Don't share passwords, payment card numbers or health information. The assistant will never ask for them.
